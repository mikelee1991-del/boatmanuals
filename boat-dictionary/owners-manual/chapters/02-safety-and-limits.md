# OM-SAFE — Safety & operating limits

## Capacity & certification

- Operate within CE category conditions for **C** / **D** as marked on the builder plate.
- Do not exceed **10 persons** or **1285 kg** total load (persons + gear + consumables as defined on the plate / CE handbook).
- Max engine certification for the hull family is **350 HP**; this boat has **300 HP** Verado — within limit.

## Always before start

1. **Lanyard / kill switch** attached to operator (**CONFIRMED** red lanyard on DTS).
2. Ventilation / no gasoline vapors in bilge before cranking.
3. Clear prop and swim platform of people, lines, tow ropes.
4. Know location of fire extinguisher and manual bilge pump.

## Carbon monoxide & exhaust

Outboard exhaust can still accumulate in cockpit/cabin in certain wind/idle conditions. Do not occupy enclosed spaces with engine idling for long periods. Maintain cabin ventilation.

## Fuel system

- Tank capacity **340 L**.
- US boat carries **EPA EVAP** compliance plate — maintain evaporative system integrity (caps, hoses, carbon canister if fitted).
- Stop engine and extinguish flames before fueling; no smoking.

## Electrical safety

- Diagram warning: **TURN OFF POWER BEFORE SERVICING.**
- Shore system includes **30 mA** RCD (Schneider iDPN N Vigi C32) — never bypass.
- After water ingress into electrical spaces: disconnect shore and batteries before drying/inspection.
- Blue Sea **VHF** fuse circuit exists but **no fixed VHF** is installed — do not assume a DSC fixed radio is available for distress.

## Steering & control

- Steering is **electro-hydraulic (EPHS)**, not pure steer-by-wire. Loss of assist increases helm effort — slow down, use both hands, return to dock.
- Keep EPHS fluid between MIN and MAX with Mercury-specified fluid only.
- If DTS alarms or shifts fail: stay in **NEUTRAL**, use **STOP**, deploy anchor if needed, call for assistance.

## Trim & stability

- **Zipwake** interceptors change running attitude. In following seas or when learning the boat, understand AUTO vs manual modes (see Zipwake operator manual).
- **LENCO is NOT INSTALLED** — do not follow LENCO procedures.

## Communications

- Primary radio: **handheld VHF** only.
- Carry charged spare battery / power bank for handheld.
- Cell coverage is not a substitute for VHF in coastal distress where DSC/coast guard watch is expected — consider adding a fixed DSC VHF later (pre-wire fuse present).

## Towing / ski pylon

- Stainless ski/tow pylon **CONFIRMED**. Use only for intended water-sports tow loads; inspect fasteners; keep observers clear of rope snap-back.

## Teak decks

- Real teak can be slippery when wet/oily — use appropriate footwear; rinse salt.
