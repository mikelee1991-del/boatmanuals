## What it does
Pair of interceptors on the transom modulate boat pitch and roll faster than traditional trim tabs. AUTO modes use GPS/IMU (per Zipwake design) to stabilize running attitude.

## Daily use
1. Power with house/helm DC.
2. Prefer **AUTO PITCH** + **AUTO ROLL** once underway and system healthy.
3. Use manual/rotary for docking or special conditions per Zipwake operator manual.
4. After grounding or trailer loading, visually check interceptor blades for damage/obstruction.

## Integration notes
- May need GPS speed/quality for full AUTO performance — Garmin GPS typically supplies network data (**UNVERIFIED** exact NMEA wiring).
- Engine trim (Verado) is separate from Zipwake interceptors; Active Trim (Mercury) may also run — understand both so they are not fighting each other.
