## What the QRG covers (Single Handle ERC)
- Engine startup / Smart Start
- **THROTTLE ONLY**
- **TRANSFER** (helm transfer — multi-station; usually unused on single-helm boats)
- **ACTIVE TRIM** on/off + profiles 1–5
- **QUICK STEER** (if equipped — single-outboard electric steering)
- Brightness, trim rocker, mechanical lock bar, START/STOP
