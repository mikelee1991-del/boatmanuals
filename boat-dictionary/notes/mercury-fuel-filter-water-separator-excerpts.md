## Short answer (this vessel)

Your Mercury **Verado 300** has an **engine-mounted water‑separating low‑pressure fuel filter**.

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Where is it? | Under the **top cowl**, **starboard aft** side of the engine, **near the bottom spark plug** |
| How do I know it needs service? | **Water‑in‑fuel alarm** / Guardian warning; or visible water/contaminants when inspected; also on the maintenance schedule (low‑pressure fuel filter icon) |
| Boat-mounted filter too? | Mercury also mentions a possible **boat-mounted** water‑separating filter. **Not yet confirmed** on HIN `BEYFT208F223` — check the fuel line between tank and engine |
| Related fuse/wiring? | Sensor harness to water‑in‑fuel sensor on the filter (or on boat-mounted filter if that is what is wired) |

## When to replace / service

- Service as general maintenance when the **water‑in‑fuel alarm** activates.
- Inspection item on Mercury schedule: check engine-mounted filter for water/contaminants; replace if required. Also check any boat-mounted separator — drain water or replace filter if required.
- Seasonal / “every X hours” items: follow the maintenance schedule decal on the engine (includes low‑pressure fuel filter icon).

## Replace outline (engine-mounted filter)

**Safety:** Key OFF, lanyard positioned so engine cannot start; no smoking/sparks; ventilate; collect fuel in approved container; check for leaks before restart.

1. Key OFF; disconnect battery before fuel-system service (Mercury fuel-system precautions).
2. Remove **top cowl**.
3. Locate filter: **starboard aft**, near **bottom spark plug**.
4. Push in fuel hose **release tabs**; disconnect hoses.
5. Disconnect **water‑in‑fuel sensor harness** (note: if a boat-mounted filter has the sensor, harness may go there instead).
6. Rotate filter **clockwise ~1/4 turn** and lift out; empty into approved container.
7. Install: light 2-4-C with PTFE on grommet; install and rotate **counterclockwise 1/4 turn**; reconnect sensor + hoses until locked.
8. Key to RUN; **inspect for leaks** before starting.
